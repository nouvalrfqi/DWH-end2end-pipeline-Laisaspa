# Business Understanding & Requirement Gathering

**Project:** End-to-End Modern Data Platform for Spa & Wellness Business

## 1. Latar Belakang

Proyek ini dibangun untuk mensimulasikan kebutuhan data pada sebuah bisnis spa dan wellness yang memiliki proses operasional harian, mulai dari booking treatment, penjualan produk, membership, gift card, konsultasi customer, hingga review layanan.

Karena ini adalah proyek personal, maka kebutuhan stakeholder akan diasumsikan secara realistis berdasarkan peran umum dalam bisnis spa, yaitu:

* **Owner / Business Manager**
* **Admin / Front Office**
* **Finance / Revenue Analyst**
* **Marketing / CRM Analyst**

Tujuan utama platform data ini adalah mengubah data operasional dari sistem POS spa menjadi informasi bisnis yang siap dianalisis, sehingga stakeholder dapat mengambil keputusan yang lebih cepat, akurat, dan berbasis data.

## 2. Tujuan Bisnis

Platform data ini dirancang untuk menjawab pertanyaan bisnis berikut:

1. Seberapa besar revenue harian, mingguan, dan bulanan?
2. Treatment dan produk apa yang paling laris?
3. Bagaimana performa booking, cancel, no-show, dan completed order?
4. Siapa customer yang paling sering datang kembali?
5. Seberapa efektif membership dalam meningkatkan repeat visit?
6. Bagaimana performa penjualan gift card?
7. Seberapa puas customer terhadap layanan spa?
8. Kapan jam atau hari paling ramai untuk booking?
9. Apakah data operasional sudah bersih, konsisten, dan siap dianalisis?

## 10. Data Source Mapping

Bagian ini menjelaskan hubungan antara tabel sumber pada database OLTP dengan kebutuhan analitik yang akan dibangun pada pipeline data.

### A. Source-to-Analytics Mapping

| Source Table        | Fungsi di OLTP           | Dipakai untuk Analitik                                                        | Target Layer                                   |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `treatments`        | Master data treatment    | Analisis treatment terlaris, revenue per treatment, active/inactive treatment | Raw → Staging → Dim Treatment                  |
| `spa_products`      | Master data produk spa   | Analisis produk terlaris, revenue produk, quantity sold                       | Raw → Staging → Dim Product                    |
| `site_settings`     | Config aplikasi          | Referensi harga membership, kategori, konfigurasi bisnis                      | Raw → Staging → Reference Layer                |
| `booking_groups`    | Header booking customer  | Analisis customer booking, customer profile, booking session summary          | Raw → Staging → Dim Customer / Fact Booking    |
| `booking_logs`      | Item booking per tamu    | Analisis status booking, booking volume, jadwal layanan, service flow         | Raw → Staging → Fact Booking                   |
| `spa_consultations` | Form konsultasi per tamu | Analisis kondisi customer, kebutuhan layanan, service personalization         | Raw → Staging → Fact Consultation              |
| `reviews`           | Review customer          | Analisis kepuasan customer, average rating, rating trend                      | Raw → Staging → Fact Review                    |
| `transactions`      | Data pembayaran terpadu  | Revenue analysis, payment summary, revenue by source                          | Raw → Staging → Fact Transaction               |
| `completed_items`   | Detail item yang dibayar | Revenue detail per item, item-level payment history                           | Raw → Staging → Fact Transaction Detail        |
| `members`           | Data membership customer | Retention, repeat visit, membership expiry, customer loyalty                  | Raw → Staging → Dim Member                     |
| `gift_cards`        | Data pembelian gift card | Gift card conversion, voucher status, revenue from voucher sales              | Raw → Staging → Dim Gift Card / Fact Gift Card |

### B. Analytic Role per Table Group

#### 1. Master Data

* `treatments`
* `spa_products`
* `site_settings`

Digunakan sebagai referensi utama untuk nama item, harga, kategori, dan konfigurasi bisnis.

#### 2. Booking & Service Flow

* `booking_groups`
* `booking_logs`
* `spa_consultations`
* `reviews`

Digunakan untuk membaca perjalanan customer dari booking sampai service selesai dan review diberikan.

#### 3. Revenue & Transaction

* `transactions` → tabel utama yang mencatat seluruh aktivitas transaksi secara **end-to-end (real revenue source)**, termasuk pembelian treatment, produk, membership, dan gift card, serta menjadi sumber utama untuk dashboard revenue di website.
* `completed_items` → tabel detail yang hanya mencatat item yang sudah berhasil diselesaikan (completed), khusus untuk **pembelian treatment dan produk saja**, tanpa mencakup gift card, dan digunakan untuk analisis granular item-level transaksi serta validasi layanan yang sudah benar-benar dilakukan.

Digunakan untuk menghitung pendapatan dan mendetailkan item yang dibayar.

#### 4. Customer & Loyalty

* members
* gift_cards: produk yang dijual di spa dalam bentuk voucher dengan nominal tertentu atau paket produk, yang dapat dibeli oleh customer, diberikan ke orang lain, dan dapat diredeem kapan saja oleh penerima sesuai ketentuan masa berlaku yang ditetapkan oleh sistem spa.

Digunakan untuk analisis loyalitas, membership, dan gift card.

## 11. KPI Formula

Bagian ini menjelaskan rumus KPI yang akan digunakan di dashboard dan data mart.

### A. Revenue KPI Formula

#### 1. Total Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Total Revenue = SUM(transactions.total_price)
```

#### 2. Revenue per Day

**Formula:**

```text
Revenue per Day = SUM(total_price) GROUP BY date(created_at)
```

#### 3. Revenue per Month

**Formula:**

```text
Revenue per Month = SUM(total_price) GROUP BY month(created_at)
```

#### 4. Revenue by Source

**Formula:**

```text
Revenue by Source = SUM(total_price) WHERE source IN ('treatment', 'product', 'membership', 'gift_card')
```

#### 5. Average Order Value (AOV)

**Formula:**

```text
AOV = Total Revenue / Total Transactions
```

#### 6. Discount Amount

**Formula:**

```text
Discount Amount = SUM(discount_amount)
```

#### 7. Net Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Net Revenue = SUM(total_price)
```

Catatan: jika `total_price` sudah net setelah diskon, maka `Net Revenue = Total Revenue`.

---

### B. Booking KPI Formula

#### 1. Total Booking

**Formula:**

```text
Total Booking = COUNT(booking_logs.id)
```

#### 2. Completed Booking Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Completed Booking Rate = COUNT(status = 'completed') / Total Booking * 100%
```

#### 3. Cancel Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Cancel Rate = COUNT(status = 'cancelled') / Total Booking * 100%
```

#### 4. No-show Rate

**Formula:**

```text
No-show Rate = COUNT(status = 'no_show') / Total Booking * 100%
```

#### 5. Arrival Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Arrival Rate = COUNT(status = 'arrived' OR status = 'completed') / Total Booking * 100%
```

#### 6. Booking per Status

**Formula:**

```text
Booking per Status = COUNT(booking_logs.id) GROUP BY status
```

---

### C. Customer KPI Formula

#### 1. Total Customer

**Formula:**

```text
Total Customer = COUNT(DISTINCT booking_groups.customer_phone)
```

#### 2. New Customer

**Formula:**

```text
New Customer = COUNT(customer_phone dengan booking pertama)
```

#### 3. Repeat Customer

**Formula:**

```text
Repeat Customer = COUNT(customer_phone dengan booking lebih dari 1 kali)
```

#### 4. Customer Retention Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Customer Retention Rate = Repeat Customer / Total Customer * 100%
```

#### 5. Average Spending per Customer

**Formula:**

```text
Average Spending per Customer = Total Revenue / Total Customer
```

#### 6. Top Spending Customer

**Formula:**

```text
Top Spending Customer = Customer dengan SUM(total_price) tertinggi
```

---

### D. Treatment & Product KPI Formula

#### 1. Top Treatment by Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Top Treatment by Revenue = SUM(price) GROUP BY treatment_name ORDER BY DESC
```

#### 2. Top Treatment by Booking Count

**Formula:**

```text
Top Treatment by Booking Count = COUNT(booking_logs) GROUP BY treatment_name ORDER BY DESC
```

#### 3. Top Product by Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Top Product by Revenue = SUM(price) GROUP BY product_name ORDER BY DESC
```

#### 4. Top Product by Quantity

**Formula:**

```text
Top Product by Quantity = COUNT(product_id) GROUP BY product_name ORDER BY DESC
```

#### 5. Inactive Treatment/Product Count

**Formula:**

```text
Inactive Count = COUNT(is_active = false)
```

---

### E. Membership KPI Formula

#### 1. Total Member

**Formula:**

```text
Total Member = COUNT(members.id)
```

#### 2. Active Member

**Formula:**

```text
Active Member = COUNT(is_active = true)
```

#### 3. Inactive Member

**Formula:**

```text
Inactive Member = COUNT(is_active = false)
```

#### 4. Membership Conversion Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Membership Conversion Rate = COUNT(customers who become member) / COUNT(total customers) * 100%
```

#### 5. Membership Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Membership Revenue = SUM(transactions.total_price WHERE source = 'membership')
```

#### 6. Membership Expiry Tracking

**Formula:**

```text
Expired Member Count = COUNT(membership_expiry < CURRENT_DATE)
```

#### 7. Total Visits per Member

**Formula:**

```text
Total Visits per Member = members.total_visits
```

#### 8. Membership Renewal Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Membership Renewal Rate = COUNT(member renewed after expiry) / COUNT(member expired) * 100%
```

---

### F. Gift Card KPI Formula

#### 1. Total Gift Card Sold

**Formula:**

```text
Total Gift Card Sold = COUNT(gift_cards.id)
```

#### 2. Gift Card Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Gift Card Revenue = SUM(transactions.total_price WHERE source = 'gift_card')
```

#### 3. Gift Card Conversion Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Gift Card Conversion Rate = COUNT(gift_cards completed) / COUNT(gift_cards booked) * 100%
```

#### 4. Active / Redeemed / Expired Gift Card

**Formula:**

```text
Active = COUNT(voucher_status = 'active')
Redeemed = COUNT(voucher_status = 'redeemed')
Expired = COUNT(voucher_status = 'expired')
```

#### 5. Top Gift Card Type

**Formula:**

```text
Top Gift Card Type = COUNT(type) GROUP BY type ORDER BY DESC
```

---

### G. Review KPI Formula

#### 1. Average Rating

**Formula:**

```text
Average Rating = AVG(reviews.rating)
```

#### 2. Rating Distribution

**Formula:**

```text
Rating Distribution = COUNT(rating) GROUP BY rating
```

#### 3. Low-rating Count

**Formula:**

```text
Low-rating Count = COUNT(rating <= 2)
```

#### 4. Review per Booking Group

**Formula:**

```text
Review per Booking Group = COUNT(review.id) GROUP BY booking_group_id
```

#### 5. Customer Satisfaction Trend

**Formula:**

```text
Customer Satisfaction Trend = AVG(rating) GROUP BY month(created_at)
```
